// What the service does

Recruiter Spam Filter is a Gmail add-on that automatically classifies and archives recruiter outreach emails. It runs inside your Google account via Google Apps Script and uses a third-party AI API to classify email content.

// Your data

Email subject lines and a portion of email body text are sent to a third-party AI classification service for the sole purpose of determining whether an email is recruiter spam. No email content is stored by us. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
