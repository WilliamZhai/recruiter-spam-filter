// Why this exists

Built for tech professionals.

The problem

AI recruiting tools now let recruiters send thousands of hyper-personalized cold emails at scale. Tech professionals get buried. Gmail's built-in filters can't keep up.

The solution

State-of-the-art AI that understands context — not just keywords. It knows the difference between a recruiter pitch and a real email, even when they look similar.

Privacy first

Email content is only sent to an AI classifier for analysis and never stored. No database, no backend — everything runs inside your own Google account.

Free to use

No subscription, no credit card. Install and go. We handle the AI costs so you don't have to think about it.
